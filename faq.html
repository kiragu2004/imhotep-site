FAQ

Everything you might want to know.

What is Imhotep?
Imhotep is a multi-agent AI assistant built by Samuel Kiragu Kamau in Murang'a, Kenya. It combines 22 specialist agents with real scientific tools (RDKit, PubMed, NASA, Qiskit, and more). Every factual claim is cited with a real source URL.
How do I start using it?
Sign in with your Google account, then choose a plan. Your chat unlocks immediately after payment.
How does payment work?
We use NestLink for M-Pesa payments. Click any plan — it opens a secure NestLink page. Enter your M-Pesa number and confirm the prompt on your phone. Credits apply automatically within 1–2 minutes.
What are the available plans?
Quick (10 KES / 3 chats), Weekly Unlimited (99 KES / 7 days), Pro Monthly (999 KES / 30 days), Pro Annual (9,999 KES / year), and Enterprise (custom).
Do my credits expire?
Quick credits never expire. Weekly and Pro plans are time-based; unused time at expiry doesn't roll over.
Is my data private?
Yes. Your sign-in uses Google OAuth. Chat history is stored only in your own browser — never on our servers.
Can the AI install its own tools?
Yes. Inside its Linux sandbox, Imhotep can install packages via apt, pip, and npm, then use them — all automatically. It only installs legitimate research and development tools.
What happens if a payment doesn't appear?
Wait 2 minutes, then click "I've paid — check now" on the chat screen. If it still doesn't appear, email kiragukamau2004@gmail.com with your M-Pesa confirmation code.
Do you offer refunds?
If no chats have been used on your account, contact support within 24 hours for a full refund.
Can I get an Enterprise plan?
Yes. Email kiragukamau2004@gmail.com with your use case. Plans start from 9,999 KES/month.
Is the AI safe?
Yes. Every request passes a safety filter. Pathogen enhancement, harmful chemistry, and cyber-attack content are refused and logged. See our Safety page for details.
Who built this?
Samuel Kiragu Kamau — alone, in his bedroom in Mukuyu, Murang'a, Kenya. See About.
