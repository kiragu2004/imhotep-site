Frequently asked questions

How do I pay?
Click any plan. It opens NestLink's secure payment page. Enter your M-Pesa number, confirm the prompt on your phone, and return here. Credits apply automatically within 1–2 minutes.
What is a "chat message"?
One question and its answer counts as one chat message. Long answers and follow-ups each count separately.
Do my credits expire?
Quick (10 KES) credits never expire. Weekly and Pro plans are time-based — unused time at expiry doesn't roll over.
Is my data private?
Yes. Your sign-in is handled by Google. Chat history is stored only in your own browser, never on our servers.
What if a payment doesn't show up?
Wait 2 minutes, then click "I've paid — check now". If it still doesn't appear, email kiragukamau2004@gmail.com with your M-Pesa confirmation code.
Can I get a refund?
If we haven't delivered any chats on your account, contact support within 24 hours for a refund.
Is there an Enterprise plan?
Yes. For team access, SLAs, or volume pricing, email kiragukamau2004@gmail.com. Plans start from 9,999 KES/month.
