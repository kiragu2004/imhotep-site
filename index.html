Built in Mukuyu, Murang'a — for Kenya

I built Imhotep because I was tired of AI that didn't understand Sheng, didn't know my county, and didn't care about Kenyan problems.

Imhotep speaks Sheng. It knows all 47 counties. It shows live NASA satellite data on fires burning in Turkana, drought conditions in Marsabit, and rainfall in your own village. It tracks how Kenya is warming — from 23.96°C in 1985 to a projected 25.19°C by 2050.

This is not an American AI with a Kenyan flag on it. This is built here, for here. Free to start. No credit card. No VPN. Just open it and say "Sasa."

🚀 VERSION 2 — IN DEVELOPMENT ● RESEARCH PREVIEW SEEKING COLLABORATORS

Imhotep V2 — Autonomous Village OS

Version 1 proved that a small, focused AI can understand Kenya. Version 2 expands that into a team of autonomous agents that monitor Kenyan climate, agriculture, and infrastructure — and act on it without waiting for human input.

What Version 2 Will Do

  • Self-training climate models — learns from new Turkana fires, Marsabit drought, and Murang'a rainfall without manual retraining
  • Multi-agent decision swarm — separate agents for data gathering, translation, farmer advisory, and fact-checking that debate before recommending
  • Automated data pipelines — pulls from NASA FIRMS, Kenya Meteorological Department, soil maps, and market prices to generate daily reports
  • GitHub auto-workflows — agents commit code, open pull requests, and run tests autonomously
  • Email agent — reads and replies in Sheng, Kiswahili, or English; sends "Mvua inakuja kesho" alerts to farmers
  • Offline deployment — runs on cheap refurbished laptops in Mukuyu cyber cafés, schools, and libraries
  • SMS and WhatsApp delivery — climate alerts for farmers without smartphones
  • Voice interface — Kikuyu, Sheng, and Kiswahili voice input and output
